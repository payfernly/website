---
title: New hire reporting
description: Telling the state about every new employee, usually within 20 days of their start date.
---

New hire reporting means sending a state agency basic details about each new employee: their name, address, Social Security number and start date, along with your business details. Federal law requires it within 20 days of hire, and some states set a shorter deadline. States use the reports to enforce child support orders and catch unemployment fraud.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

It's a small filing that's easy to forget, especially when you hire in a state you haven't paid in before. States can fine you for each late report. Building it into your onboarding checklist, or letting your payroll provider do it, keeps it from slipping.
