title: PFML
description: Paid family and medical leave: a state program that pays workers during qualifying time off.
---

PFML stands for paid family and medical leave. It's the name several states, including Massachusetts and Washington, use for their state-run leave insurance programs. Like Colorado's [FAMLI](/glossary/famli), they pay workers part of their wages during qualifying leave, funded by contributions withheld from pay and, in many states, an employer share.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Every PFML state has its own registration, contribution rate, reporting schedule and notice requirements, and rates often change each year. Several states reduce or waive the employer share for small businesses, but you usually still have to register and withhold the employee share. Hiring one remote employee in a PFML state is enough to bring you in.
