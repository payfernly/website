Multi-state payroll means paying employees who live or work in different states. Each state has its own income tax withholding, unemployment tax, paid leave programs, minimum wage and pay rules, and some cities and counties add their own taxes. Some neighboring states have reciprocity agreements that change which state you withhold for.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

For a small team, the second state is the hard part: before the first paycheck there, you may need several new registrations with several agencies. The math is rarely the problem; the paperwork and deadlines are. Missing one usually surfaces months later as a penalty notice.
