---
title: Net pay
description: What actually lands in an employee's bank account after taxes and deductions.
---

Net pay is what an employee takes home: [gross pay](/glossary/gross-pay) minus taxes withheld and any [deductions](/glossary/deduction), such as health insurance or retirement contributions. It's the number that shows up in their bank account on payday. Most people call it take-home pay.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Net pay is the number your team notices. If it's lower than they expected, they'll ask why, and you need to be able to point to each line on the [pay stub](/glossary/pay-stub). At a small company there's no payroll department to field those questions, so the clearer the math, the fewer interruptions you get.
