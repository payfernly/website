---
title: Contractor vs. employee
description: The legal line between someone who works for you independently and someone you employ.
---

An employee works under your direction, and you withhold taxes and pay [payroll tax](/glossary/payroll-tax) on their wages. An independent contractor runs their own business, controls how the work is done, and handles their own taxes. You report what you pay contractors on a [1099-NEC](/glossary/1099-nec).

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

The label you choose doesn't decide it; the working relationship does. The IRS looks at behavioral control, financial control and the relationship itself, and some states, including California, Massachusetts and New Jersey, use a stricter test. Misclassifying an employee as a contractor can mean back taxes, penalties and missed benefits.
