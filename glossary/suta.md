---
title: SUTA
description: State unemployment tax, paid to fund unemployment benefits in the state where someone works.
---

SUTA is state unemployment tax. Each state runs its own unemployment insurance program and charges employers a rate on a set amount of each employee's wages. New employers usually get a standard rate for their first year or two, and after that the rate depends partly on how many former employees have claimed benefits.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

You need a separate unemployment account in every state where you have employees, often with a different agency from income tax withholding. Paying SUTA on time also keeps your [FUTA](/glossary/futa) credit intact. A few states also withhold a small amount from employees' pay, which is easy to miss when you hire there for the first time.
