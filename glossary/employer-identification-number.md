title: Employer identification number (EIN)
description: The nine-digit federal tax ID the IRS assigns to a business.
---

An employer identification number (EIN) is a nine-digit number the IRS assigns to identify a business for tax purposes. It works like a Social Security number for your company. You apply for one with the IRS, online and for free.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

You need an EIN before you can run payroll, register for [state tax IDs](/glossary/state-tax-id), or file [Form 941](/glossary/form-941). It appears on every [W-2](/glossary/w-2) and 1099 you send. If your business changes structure, for example from a sole proprietorship to a corporation, you may need a new one.
