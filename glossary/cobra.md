---
title: COBRA
description: The federal law that lets employees keep their group health coverage for a time after leaving a job.
---

COBRA is a federal law that lets employees and their families stay on the company's group health plan after a qualifying event, such as leaving the job or having hours cut. They usually pay the full premium themselves, plus a small administrative fee. Coverage typically lasts up to 18 months, and longer in some cases.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Federal COBRA applies to employers with 20 or more employees, but many states have their own continuation laws, often called mini-COBRA, that cover smaller groups. Either way, there are strict deadlines for sending notices when someone leaves, and missing them can bring penalties. Departures are already busy; a missed notice is easy to overlook.
