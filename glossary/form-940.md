Form 940 is the Employer's Annual Federal Unemployment Tax Return. It reports the [FUTA](/glossary/futa) tax you owe on employee wages for the year and any deposits you already made. It's due January 31, or February 10 if every deposit was made on time.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Form 940 only comes around once a year, so it's easy to forget until January. Its numbers depend on your state unemployment payments, and any state that has borrowed from the federal government can raise what you owe. It's one of several filings that land in the same few weeks of [year-end payroll](/glossary/year-end-payroll).
