---
title: Gross pay
description: Everything an employee earns in a pay period before taxes and deductions come out.
---

Gross pay is what an employee earns in a pay period before anything is taken out. For salaried staff it's their salary divided by the number of pay periods in the year. For hourly staff it's hours worked times their rate, plus any [overtime](/glossary/overtime), bonuses, commissions or tips.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Almost every payroll number starts from gross pay: [FICA](/glossary/fica), [federal income tax withholding](/glossary/federal-income-tax-withholding), state taxes and benefit [deductions](/glossary/deduction) are all calculated from it. Get gross pay wrong, say by missing approved overtime hours, and every number after it is wrong too. With 5 to 50 people, one mistake usually means an off-cycle correction and an awkward conversation.

Fernly runs weekly, bi-weekly and semi-monthly pay runs, plus one-off bonuses. [See payroll](/features/payroll).
