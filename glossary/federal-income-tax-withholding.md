title: Federal income tax withholding

---

Federal income tax withholding is the amount an employer takes from each paycheck toward the employee's federal income tax bill. The amount depends on the employee's pay, pay frequency and the choices they made on their [W-4](/glossary/w-4). The employer deposits it with the IRS and reports it each quarter on [Form 941](/glossary/form-941).

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Once you withhold it, that money is held in trust for the IRS, and the IRS treats unpaid withholding seriously. Owners can be held personally liable when it isn't deposited. Using an out-of-date W-4 or the wrong tables leaves employees owing money at tax time, and they'll come to you about it.
