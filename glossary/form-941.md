Form 941 is the Employer's Quarterly Federal Tax Return. Four times a year it tells the IRS how much you paid employees, how much [federal income tax](/glossary/federal-income-tax-withholding) you withheld, and how much [FICA](/glossary/fica) you and your employees owe. It's due April 30, July 31, October 31 and January 31.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

The 941 has to match the tax deposits you made during the quarter. If it doesn't, the IRS sends a notice, often with a penalty attached. Small teams run into trouble with bonuses reported in the wrong quarter and with using the wrong deposit schedule.
