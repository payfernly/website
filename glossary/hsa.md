A health savings account (HSA) lets employees set aside pre-tax money for medical expenses. To contribute, they must be enrolled in a qualifying high-deductible health plan. The account belongs to the employee, the balance rolls over every year, and it goes with them if they leave.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Contributions made through payroll skip income tax and [FICA](/glossary/fica), which saves money for both the employee and you. You can also add employer contributions, which many small teams use to soften a higher deductible. The IRS sets annual contribution limits, so payroll has to stop deductions once someone hits theirs.
