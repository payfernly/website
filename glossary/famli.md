---
title: FAMLI
description: Colorado's paid family and medical leave insurance program, funded through payroll.
---

FAMLI is Colorado's Paid Family and Medical Leave Insurance program. It pays eligible workers a share of their wages while they take time off to bond with a new child, recover from a serious illness or care for a family member. It's funded by a premium on wages, split between employer and employee.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

If you have even one employee in Colorado, you need to register with FAMLI and report wages every quarter. Businesses with fewer than 10 employees don't have to pay the employer half of the premium, but they still have to register and send in the employee half. A missing FAMLI registration is the kind of thing that's easy to fix on a Tuesday and expensive to fix after a penalty notice.
