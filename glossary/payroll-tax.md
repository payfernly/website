title: Payroll tax

---

Payroll tax is the umbrella term for taxes calculated on wages. Some are withheld from employees' pay, such as [federal income tax withholding](/glossary/federal-income-tax-withholding) and the employee half of [FICA](/glossary/fica). Others are paid by the employer on top of wages, such as the employer half of FICA, [FUTA](/glossary/futa) and [SUTA](/glossary/suta).

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Payroll taxes come with deposit deadlines, quarterly returns and annual forms at both the federal and state level. Late or missed deposits bring penalties and interest, even when the mistake is small. For a team of 5 to 50, the math is rarely the problem; keeping track of every agency and due date is.

Fernly calculates, pays and files federal, state and local payroll taxes. [See compliance](/features/compliance).
