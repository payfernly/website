---
title: W-2
description: The annual form showing an employee's wages and the taxes withheld from them.
---

Form W-2 is the year-end summary of an employee's pay. It shows total wages, federal and state income tax withheld, [FICA](/glossary/fica) and some benefits. Employers send a copy to each employee and to the Social Security Administration by January 31.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Your team needs their W-2 to file their personal tax returns, so a late or wrong one is noticed fast. The totals also have to match what you reported on your four quarterly [Form 941](/glossary/form-941) returns. W-2s are only for employees; contractors get a [1099-NEC](/glossary/1099-nec) instead.
