---
title: Paid family leave
description: Paid time off to bond with a new child, care for a family member or recover from illness.
---

Paid family leave is time off with partial pay for major life events, such as a birth or adoption, caring for a seriously ill family member, or recovering from a serious health condition. There's no federal requirement to provide it. A growing number of states run their own programs, funded through payroll, under names like [FAMLI](/glossary/famli) and [PFML](/glossary/pfml).

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

States with programs include Colorado, Washington, Oregon, Massachusetts, Connecticut, New Jersey, New York, Rhode Island, California, Delaware, Maryland, Maine and Minnesota. Each one means a separate registration and its own deductions on the [pay stub](/glossary/pay-stub). If your team spans several of these states, keeping track of them is a job in itself.
