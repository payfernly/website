---
title: Remote workforce payroll
description: Paying employees who work from home or from a different state than your office.
---

Remote workforce payroll is running payroll for people who don't work at your office. In general, wages are taxed where the employee physically does the work, so a remote employee in another state usually means registering and withholding there. A few states, including New York, can tax remote employees of in-state employers under their own rules.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Remote hiring lets a small company hire anywhere in the country, but every new state brings its own taxes, [paid leave](/glossary/paid-family-leave) programs and wage rules. When an employee moves, their payroll setup has to move with them. Ask your team to tell you before they relocate, not after.
