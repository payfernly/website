---
title: Payroll service bureau
description: An outside firm that processes payroll for you using the information you send it.
---

A payroll service bureau is an outside firm that processes payroll on your behalf. You send it hours and changes each pay period, and it calculates pay, handles deposits and files payroll taxes under your business's tax IDs. Many are local firms or accounting practices.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

A bureau takes the processing off your plate while you stay the employer. Because you're relaying details to someone else, timing and accuracy depend on how quickly changes get passed along. Ask what they file, what they charge per run or per form, and who fixes errors.
