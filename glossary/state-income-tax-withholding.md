---
title: State income tax withholding
description: The state income tax an employer takes out of paychecks in states that tax wages.
---

State income tax withholding works like federal withholding, but for the state where the employee works or lives. Most states tax wages, and each has its own forms, rates and filing schedule. Nine states, including Texas, Florida and Washington, don't tax wages at all.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

You usually have to register for a state withholding account before you run payroll for someone in that state, and some states take weeks to issue the account number. If you have people in more than one state, you may need to withhold for the state they work in, the state they live in, or both. That's where small teams most often get caught out.
