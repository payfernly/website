---
title: PTO accrual
description: How employees build up paid time off over time, such as a few hours per pay period.
---

PTO accrual is the way employees earn paid time off over time, instead of getting it all at once. A common setup gives a set number of hours each [pay period](/glossary/pay-period) or for every hour worked, up to a cap. The balance goes down when they take time off.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Many states and cities require paid sick leave, with their own minimum accrual rates and carryover rules. Some states, including California, treat earned vacation as wages that must be paid out when someone leaves. Your PTO policy needs to meet the rules in every state where your team works.
