---
title: Payroll register
description: A report listing every employee's pay, taxes and deductions for a pay run.
---

A payroll register is the record of a single pay run. For each employee it lists hours, [gross pay](/glossary/gross-pay), each tax withheld, each [deduction](/glossary/deduction) and [net pay](/glossary/net-pay), with totals for the company. Think of it as every [pay stub](/glossary/pay-stub) from that payday on one page.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

The register is what your accountant uses to book payroll and what you compare against your bank account and tax filings during [payroll reconciliation](/glossary/payroll-reconciliation). Labor departments can ask for payroll records going back years. Keeping a clean register for every run makes those requests a quick download rather than a scramble.
