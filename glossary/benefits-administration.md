Benefits administration is the day-to-day work of running employee benefits: enrolling new hires, processing changes, removing people who leave, and making sure the right [deductions](/glossary/deduction) come out of each paycheck. It covers health, dental and vision insurance, retirement plans and pre-tax accounts like an [HSA](/glossary/hsa) or [FSA](/glossary/fsa).

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

At a small company, benefits administration often lands on the founder or office manager, on top of everything else. The usual problem is drift: a carrier change that never reaches payroll, so deductions are wrong for months. Keeping benefits and payroll in one system means there's one record to keep right.

Fernly is a licensed benefits broker in all 50 states. Fernly syncs health, dental, vision, 401(k) and FSA deductions from enrollment straight into payroll. [See benefits](/features/benefits).
