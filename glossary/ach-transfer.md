An ACH transfer is an electronic payment between US bank accounts, sent over the Automated Clearing House network. Banks process ACH payments in batches rather than one at a time, which keeps them cheap but means they take a business day or more to settle. [Direct deposit](/glossary/direct-deposit) and most payroll tax payments run on ACH.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

ACH timing sets your payroll deadlines. Weekends and bank holidays don't count as business days, so a Monday holiday can move your approval deadline earlier. If a transfer is returned, for example because of a closed account, it can take days to sort out.
