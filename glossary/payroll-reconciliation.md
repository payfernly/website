---
title: Payroll reconciliation
description: Checking that payroll records, bank withdrawals and tax filings all agree.
---

Payroll reconciliation is the process of checking that your payroll records match everything else: the money that left your bank account, the tax deposits you made, the returns you filed and your accounting books. It's usually done after each pay run and again at the end of each quarter and year.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Reconciling catches problems while they're small, like a missed tax deposit or a deduction that never reached a carrier. Your quarterly [Form 941](/glossary/form-941) has to tie out to your deposits, and your [W-2s](/glossary/w-2) have to tie out to your four 941s. Fixing a mismatch in March is far easier than answering an IRS notice in July.

Read [Form 941, explained](/blog/form-941-explained) for why your deposits have to match.
