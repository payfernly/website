title: FUTA
description: The federal unemployment tax employers pay on the first $7,000 of each employee's wages.
---

FUTA is the Federal Unemployment Tax Act tax. Employers pay it, not employees, on the first $7,000 of each employee's wages each year. The rate is 6%, but most employers get a credit of up to 5.4% for paying their state unemployment tax on time, which brings it down to 0.6%.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

FUTA is small per person, but the credit depends on paying [SUTA](/glossary/suta) on time, and employers in some states get a smaller credit if their state owes the federal government money. It's reported once a year on [Form 940](/glossary/form-940), with deposits due during the year once the amount owed passes $500.
