---
title: Deduction
description: Any amount taken out of an employee's gross pay, other than the taxes themselves.
---

A deduction is money taken out of a paycheck for something other than taxes. Pre-tax deductions, such as most [health insurance premiums](/glossary/health-insurance-premium), 401(k) contributions and [FSA](/glossary/fsa) elections, come out before taxes are calculated. Post-tax deductions, such as Roth 401(k) contributions and [garnishments](/glossary/garnishment), come out after.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Whether a deduction is pre-tax or post-tax changes the taxes on every paycheck, so getting the type wrong throws off both the employee's [net pay](/glossary/net-pay) and your tax filings. Many states also limit what you can deduct without written permission. Most deduction errors at small companies come from benefits changes that never made it into payroll.

Fernly syncs health, dental, vision, 401(k) and FSA deductions from enrollment straight into payroll. [See benefits](/features/benefits).
