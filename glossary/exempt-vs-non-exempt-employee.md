---
title: Exempt vs. non-exempt employee
description: Whether an employee is entitled to overtime pay under federal and state law.
---

Non-exempt employees are covered by [FLSA](/glossary/flsa) overtime rules, so they're paid for every hour worked and earn [overtime](/glossary/overtime) past 40 hours a week. Exempt employees aren't owed overtime. To be exempt, a role usually has to pay a salary above a set minimum and involve executive, administrative or professional duties.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Paying someone a salary doesn't make them exempt; the job duties have to qualify too. Many states set a higher salary minimum than the federal one. Getting it wrong means back pay for every overtime hour worked, which adds up quickly on a small team.
