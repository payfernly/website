title: W-4

---

Form W-4 is the IRS form an employee completes when they start a job. It tells the employer their filing status, whether they have more than one job, any dependents and any extra amount they want withheld. The employer uses it to calculate [federal income tax withholding](/glossary/federal-income-tax-withholding).

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

You need a W-4 on file before someone's first paycheck. Without one, you're required to withhold as if they're single with no adjustments. Employees can update it whenever their situation changes, and you should apply the new one by the next practical pay run. Many states have their own version for state withholding.

Fernly employees manage their own bank details, pay stubs and tax forms. [See payroll](/features/payroll).
