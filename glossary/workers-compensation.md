---
title: Workers' compensation
description: State-required insurance that covers employees' medical costs and lost wages after a work injury.
---

Workers' compensation is insurance that pays medical bills and part of lost wages when an employee is hurt or gets sick because of their job. In exchange, employees generally can't sue the employer over the injury. Almost every state requires employers to carry it, and the rules for who must be covered vary by state.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Premiums are usually based on payroll and the type of work, so accurate payroll records feed straight into your bill. Operating without required coverage can bring fines and leave you personally on the hook for an injury. When you hire in a new state, check that your policy covers employees there.
