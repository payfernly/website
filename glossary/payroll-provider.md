---
title: Payroll provider
description: A company or software that calculates pay, pays employees and handles payroll taxes for you.
---

A payroll provider is the company or software you use to run payroll. At a minimum it calculates [gross pay](/glossary/gross-pay), taxes and [net pay](/glossary/net-pay), and pays your team by [direct deposit](/glossary/direct-deposit). Most also deposit and file your payroll taxes, and some handle benefits and state registrations too.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Even when a provider files for you, the IRS still holds the employer responsible for taxes being paid. So it matters what the provider covers, how mistakes get fixed, and whether you can reach a person who knows your account. Switching providers is reasonable at any point in the year, ideally at the start of a quarter.
