---
title: Tax nexus
description: A connection to a state that's strong enough to create tax obligations there.
---

Tax nexus is the link between a business and a state that gives the state the right to tax it. For payroll, having even one employee working in a state usually creates obligations there, such as withholding income tax and paying [unemployment tax](/glossary/suta). An employee in a state can also create nexus for other taxes, like state income or sales tax.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

A single remote hire can bring a whole set of new obligations in a state where you have no office. It's worth checking with your accountant about the non-payroll taxes, too, since payroll is often the first sign a state sees that you're operating there.
