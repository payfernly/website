---
title: FICA
description: The Social Security and Medicare taxes that employers and employees each pay on wages.
---

FICA stands for the Federal Insurance Contributions Act, the law behind Social Security and Medicare taxes. Employees pay 6.2% of wages for Social Security, up to an annual wage cap, and 1.45% for Medicare with no cap. The employer pays a matching amount on top, so the total is roughly 15.3% of most wages.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

The employer half of FICA is a real cost of every hire, on top of salary. Both halves are deposited with the IRS on a set schedule and reported each quarter on [Form 941](/glossary/form-941). Missing a deposit is one of the quickest ways for a small business to pick up IRS penalties.
