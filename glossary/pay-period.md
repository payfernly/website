---
title: Pay period
description: The stretch of time a paycheck covers, such as one week, two weeks or half a month.
---

A pay period is the span of days a single paycheck covers. The common schedules are weekly (52 paychecks a year), bi-weekly (26), semi-monthly (24) and monthly (12). Payday usually falls a few days after the period ends, so there's time to collect hours and run payroll.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Many states set a minimum pay frequency, and some require hourly workers to be paid more often than monthly, so check the rules for each state you pay in. Your schedule also changes the math: a salary split across 26 bi-weekly checks looks different from one split across 24 semi-monthly checks. Picking a schedule and sticking with it keeps payroll predictable for you and your team.
