---
title: Garnishment
description: A legal order requiring an employer to withhold part of an employee's pay for a debt.
---

A wage garnishment is a court or government order telling an employer to withhold part of an employee's pay and send it to a third party. Common reasons are child support, unpaid taxes, student loans and court judgments. Federal law limits how much can be withheld, and child support orders take priority.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Once you receive an order, you're legally responsible for following it. If you don't withhold correctly, you can end up owing the amount yourself. Each order has its own calculation, payment address and deadlines, which is a lot to track for a team without a payroll department.
