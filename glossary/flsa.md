The Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) is the federal law behind minimum wage, [overtime](/glossary/overtime), recordkeeping and child labor rules. It decides which employees must be paid overtime and which are [exempt](/glossary/exempt-vs-non-exempt-employee). States and cities can set stricter rules, and when they do, the stricter rule wins.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

The FLSA applies to almost every business, no matter how small. Unpaid overtime claims can reach back two or three years and include equal amounts in damages. Keeping accurate time records and classifying roles correctly are the two habits that prevent most problems.
