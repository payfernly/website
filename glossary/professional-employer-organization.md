title: Professional employer organization (PEO)
description: A company that co-employs your team and takes on payroll, benefits and HR under its own tax IDs.
---

A professional employer organization (PEO) enters a co-employment arrangement with your business. The PEO becomes the employer for tax and benefits purposes, runs payroll under its own tax IDs, and puts your team on its benefits plans. You still direct the day-to-day work.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Small businesses often look at a PEO for access to large-group health plans. The trade-offs are less control over benefits, pricing that's often tied to headcount or payroll, and a more involved exit, since your team's coverage and tax history sit with the PEO. Compare that with keeping payroll in your own name with a [payroll provider](/glossary/payroll-provider).
