---
title: State tax ID
description: The account numbers a state assigns so you can withhold and pay its payroll taxes.
---

A state tax ID is the account number a state gives your business for payroll taxes. Most states issue at least two: one for [state income tax withholding](/glossary/state-income-tax-withholding) and one for [unemployment tax](/glossary/suta), often from different agencies. States with [paid family leave](/glossary/paid-family-leave) programs may add a third.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

You need these numbers before you can file in a state, and the wait varies from minutes to weeks depending on the state. Every new state you hire in means a new set of registrations. It's one of the most common reasons a first paycheck in a new state goes out late.
