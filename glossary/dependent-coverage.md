---
title: Dependent coverage
description: Extending an employee's health, dental or vision plan to their spouse or children.
---

Dependent coverage lets an employee add family members, usually a spouse or domestic partner and children, to their benefits plan. Under the [ACA](/glossary/aca), plans that cover children must let them stay on until age 26. Adding dependents raises the premium, and employers decide how much of that extra cost they'll pay.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

How much you contribute toward dependent coverage is one of the biggest choices in your benefits budget, and one candidates ask about. Dependents are usually added or removed during [open enrollment](/glossary/open-enrollment) or after a qualifying life event. Each change has to reach both the carrier and the payroll [deduction](/glossary/deduction).
