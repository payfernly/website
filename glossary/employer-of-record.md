title: Employer of record (EOR)
description: A company that legally employs workers on your behalf while you direct their work.
---

An employer of record (EOR) is a company that becomes the legal employer for people who work for you. It handles their contracts, payroll, taxes and benefits, while you manage their day-to-day work. Unlike a [PEO](/glossary/professional-employer-organization), which shares employer duties with you, an EOR takes them on entirely.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

EORs are mostly used to hire in places where a business has no legal entity, such as another country. For a US team paying people in the US, you can usually register in each state yourself, or have your payroll provider do it, and stay the employer. That generally costs less per person.
