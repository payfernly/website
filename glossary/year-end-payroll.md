---

Year-end payroll is the work of closing out the calendar year. It includes the fourth-quarter [Form 941](/glossary/form-941), the annual [Form 940](/glossary/form-940), [W-2s](/glossary/w-2) for employees, [1099-NECs](/glossary/1099-nec) for contractors, and state annual returns. Most of it is due by January 31.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Year-end is when small mistakes from earlier in the year show up: a wrong address, a missing Social Security number, or a bonus paid in the wrong year. Checking employee details and timing bonuses before the last pay run of December saves a lot of corrections in January.

Fernly calculates, pays and files federal, state and local payroll taxes, including quarterly Form 941 and annual W-2s, and prepares 1099-NEC forms for contractors. [See compliance](/features/compliance).
