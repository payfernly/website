---
title: Direct deposit
description: Paying employees electronically into their bank accounts instead of by paper check.
---

Direct deposit sends each employee's [net pay](/glossary/net-pay) straight into their bank account. It runs over the [ACH](/glossary/ach-transfer) network, so the money has to leave the employer's account a day or two before payday. Employees can usually split their pay across more than one account.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Direct deposit saves you a trip to the bank and your team a wait for a check to clear. The catch is timing: you need to approve payroll early enough for the transfer to arrive by payday. Some states let employees choose a paper check instead, so check the rules before making direct deposit mandatory.

Fernly delivers direct deposits in two business days, or next-day on the Plus plan. [See payroll](/features/payroll).
