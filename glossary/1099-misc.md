title: 1099-MISC
description: The IRS form for miscellaneous business payments such as rent, prizes and royalties.
---

Form 1099-MISC reports miscellaneous payments a business makes that aren't wages or contractor fees, such as rent, royalties, prizes and awards. Payments to contractors for their work used to go on this form but moved to the [1099-NEC](/glossary/1099-nec) starting with the 2020 tax year.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Small businesses still mix the two up. If you pay a contractor for services, use the 1099-NEC; if you pay rent for an office to an individual landlord, that may need a 1099-MISC. Filing the wrong form can bring IRS notices to you and confusion for the person you paid.
