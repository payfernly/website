---
title: 1099-NEC
description: The form that reports what you paid an independent contractor during the year.
---

Form 1099-NEC reports nonemployee compensation: what you paid an independent contractor for their services during the year. You send one to each contractor you paid above the IRS reporting threshold and file a copy with the IRS. Both are due January 31.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

If you work with freelancers, the 1099-NEC lands in the same week as your [W-2s](/glossary/w-2). You need each contractor's legal name, address and taxpayer ID, usually from a Form W-9 collected before you pay them. Chasing W-9s in January is a common year-end headache.
