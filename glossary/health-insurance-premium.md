---
title: Health insurance premium
description: The monthly cost of a health plan, usually shared between employer and employee.
---

A health insurance premium is the amount paid each month to keep a health plan active. With employer-sponsored coverage, the employer usually pays part of it and the employee's share comes out of their paycheck as a [deduction](/glossary/deduction). Paying that share before taxes lowers both the employee's taxable income and the employer's [payroll tax](/glossary/payroll-tax).

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Premiums are often the biggest benefits cost a small business carries, and they usually change every plan year. Many carriers require small employers to contribute a minimum share and to have a minimum number of employees enroll. Knowing those rules before [open enrollment](/glossary/open-enrollment) avoids surprises.
