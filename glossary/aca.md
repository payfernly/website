The Affordable Care Act (ACA) is the federal health care law passed in 2010. For employers, its best-known rule is the employer mandate: businesses with 50 or more full-time employees, counting full-time equivalents, must offer affordable coverage or pay a penalty. It also sets standards for what plans cover, such as letting children stay on a parent's plan until age 26.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

If you have fewer than 50 full-time employees, the ACA doesn't require you to offer health insurance. But part-time hours count toward the 50 through full-time equivalents, so a growing team can cross the line sooner than expected. Small employers that do offer coverage may also qualify for a federal tax credit.
