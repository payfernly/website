---
title: Open enrollment
description: The yearly window when employees can join, change or drop their benefits.
---

Open enrollment is the period each year when employees can sign up for benefits, switch plans, add or remove dependents, or opt out. Outside of open enrollment, changes are generally only allowed after a qualifying life event, such as marriage, a birth or losing other coverage. The window usually runs a few weeks before the new plan year starts.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Open enrollment is when your benefits costs and your payroll deductions both reset. Every election has to reach the carrier and show up as the right [deduction](/glossary/deduction) on the first paycheck of the plan year. For a small team, a clear plan comparison makes the difference between a quick week and a month of questions.

Fernly syncs health, dental, vision, 401(k) and FSA deductions from enrollment straight into payroll. [See benefits](/features/benefits).
