---
title: Overtime
description: Extra pay, usually time and a half, for non-exempt employees who work beyond set hours.
---

Overtime is the extra pay non-exempt employees earn when they work beyond a set number of hours. Under the [FLSA](/glossary/flsa), that means at least 1.5 times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek. Some states go further: California, for example, also requires overtime after eight hours in a day.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

Overtime is one of the most common sources of wage claims against small businesses. The regular rate can include bonuses and commissions, not just the hourly wage, and the rules depend on the state where the work happens. Classifying someone as [exempt](/glossary/exempt-vs-non-exempt-employee) when they don't qualify means owing them back overtime.
