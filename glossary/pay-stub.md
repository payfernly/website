---
title: Pay stub
description: The breakdown employees get each payday showing earnings, taxes, deductions and take-home pay.
---

A pay stub shows an employee how their paycheck was calculated. It lists [gross pay](/glossary/gross-pay), each tax withheld, each [deduction](/glossary/deduction) and [net pay](/glossary/net-pay), usually with year-to-date totals. Employees also use them as proof of income for loans and leases.

## Why it matters for a team of 5 to 50

There's no federal pay stub requirement, but many states require one and spell out what it must show, such as hours worked, rates and the employer's name and address. A clear pay stub answers most paycheck questions before they reach you.

Fernly employees download their own pay stubs and tax forms. [See payroll](/features/payroll).
